Chapter 8: Polariton

The chapter opens on the cold open, in which Scarletbug and Knightclaw realize they need to be more careful about what to do when they have to fight alone or can’t talk to each other.  After all, Registrar made them split up in ways that they hadn’t had to do before.  They decide to help each other in their respective weak spots: Scarletbug helps Knightclaw with planning, and Knightclaw helps Scarletbug with direct combat under pressure.  With that, they return to sparring each other.  The title sequence plays

The chapter opens with Nishant trying to reason through what exactly happened in Chapter 7’s diffusion equation test.  Ruth clearly could reproduce the Cottrell equation, could speak like she knew PDEs, and then when given one, acted like she’d never seen a partial derivative before.  Then there’s Elliot’s account in which he gave him conflicting information, or at least what seemed like it?  Then the probabilities he calculated and the probability density heatmaps from Registrar’s akumatization both suggest that this out-of-syllabus knowledge was recent and that it happened across several classes at once in the same sudden way.  He finds he’s not able to find an explanation that accounts for everything at once.  So he reasons: Ruth already had the Butterfly Miraculous, maybe he was wrong in thinking that was the only one?  If that’s true, that means that he’s missing a huge detail, and it’s worth testing, and he already knows the one place Registrar didn’t search, and where it is: Ruth’s dorm.  If she has another jewel, it’s either on her person or in her dorm, or somewhere else.  He doesn’t remember seeing Ruth with any jewel that’s not the 🌸 brooch, and in Chapter 7, he doesn’t remember seeing Ruth wearing any type of jewel at all, no brooches, no rings, no earrings, hair jewels even, or the like.  That’s consistent with what he expected at first: he has Ruth’s brooch now, so she shouldn’t have any brooches.  But if the jewel wasn’t on her person, then he would have missed it anyways.  It seems likely and worthwhile enough to check if her dorm really does have a jewel.  So he akumatizes himself into Polariton (which allows him to use either no power, invisibility only, intangibility only, but not both), to search it.  His akumatized object is left behind at his dorm room, and the weapon he carries (which is magically bound to his back) is a sword sheathed in a cane just in case the superheroes get involved.   A quick pass finds nothing.  He finds the “To Our Sweet and Perfect Ruth” card but moves on.

Meanwhile, Scarletbug and Knightclaw are on high alert regarding Ruth given just how many akumatizations have involved her at this point: Sociovestigator, Sentinella, Examinator, Registrar, and they’re already on high alert trying to keep Ruth safe anyways.  So they quickly realize that something is off when they see strange activity around Ruth’s dorm.  So they peek in, and Polariton is forced to hide using invisibility, but now he must remain completely still because the inability to use intangibility means that he can accidentally make sounds by interacting with the physical environment.  Then the door handle turns, forcing Polariton to use intangibility to escape.  This leads to him being discovered by the superheroes.  But since Polariton’s costume was designed to hide his identity in case this happens, the superheroes know an akumatized villain is around, but not who.  Polariton’s logic here: someone is approaching the dorm from the inside, heroes are inspecting from the outside.  Polariton must escape but to do that he must switch to intangibility.  But that means he will be seen.  So he must pick: “be seen by the heroes” or “be seen by Ruth”.  He picks heroes since he has a contingency that can more easily deal with that.

Polariton tries to shake them off, but the heroes are prepared.  He momentarily loses them, and tries to return to the dorm, but just sees that Ruth has no jewels on.  Now he’s confused.  He quickly retreats to avoid being seen by Ruth.  Ruth sees the superheroes fighting an akumatized villain, once again near her dorm in New House, and quickly shuts the door.  Inside, she complains to herself about not being able to be with the sentibeing (aka deploy the sentibeing) or even keep her Miraculous on her anymore, the sentibeing failing to do what it was supposed to (and it was an akumatized villain, who the audience but not her, knows by the name Registrar, who was part of that after losing the Butterfly Miraculous and the akumatized villain coming too close (this makes twice in a row!) she can quickly realize that her missing Miraculous is now in someone else’s hands instead of just simply being misplaced or lost to the protocol (if Lepidon is able to figure this out, this could be valuable for setting the Senti-Argus plan into motion when it comes), so she’s protected the Peacock Miraculous and decided not to use Senti-Ruth because it almost/did became a liability in Chapter 7.  With that, she shuts the blinds to her room, just like she did with Registrar, except this time she’s less rattled because she’s not vulnerable to exposure in the way she was during Registrar.  Lepidon of course does not know he’ll need Senti-Argus at this point, but it can still become retroactively important when he does end up coming up with the plan due to needing it somewhere after Chapter 16 (provoke Ruth into creating an Argus duplicate that she deliberately has act suspiciously).

Meanwhile Polariton finds it difficult to manage fighting both superheroes while searching at the same time (while using his powers because otherwise there’s no way to face off against two superheroes while on a search), and with Ruth now in the room, he can’t search until she leaves, so he falls back on his plan B: fight the heroes directly and learn their battle tactics.  At first, the heroes keep getting the upper hand.  But he learns to listen to Knightclaw when he gives commands (so he can predict their moves), watch Scarletbug when he can, and learn one mistake at a time.  The superheroes try to locate an akumatized object on him but they can’t find something that releases the akuma.  Breaking the can just allows Polariton to respawn one.  Once he’s gotten the hang of superpowered combat, he locks in and uses his anger, and starts gaining the upper hand on them, and fully uses his cane to fight.  He’s effectively gotten the very superheroes he’s fighting to train him in combat. Which was his fallback plan because he knows the superheroes are better at combat than he is, meaning he can use the battle as an opportunity to learn from someone more skilled than himself, and they have no other choice: their very action of fighting the akumatized villain becomes the training itself.

Knightclaw realizes that verbal commands have become a liability and drops them altogether, causing the heroes, for the first time, to fall back on a nonverbal plan.  No speaking, just coordinated fighting.  But Knightclaw recognizes this is a duel now.  So he salutes with his staff.  Polariton does not know what the gesture means but he knows it’s a gesture of formality or respect, so he draws the sword from his cane (so he’s effectively dual wielding) and returns the gesture.  Then happens a fierce battle between Polariton and the heroes where both give their all and Knightclaw finds himself actually finding the battle difficult compared to his previous ones, and Polariton is constantly learning.  But for this climatic wordless battle, Polariton does not use his powers even though he still has the choice to use them.  To him, this is him training against the very heroes he might find himself fighting as Lepidon (so bringing the cane was in preparation for this plan B), and he gets to learn from Knightclaw himself (and Scarletbug) whether they like it or not.  So the match ends in a stalemate, in which Polariton uses invisibility at the end to slip away, leaving the heroes rattled by the battle.  This marks the first battle that does not end in a forced de-akumatization.  The heroes realize that Crystal Moth (or rather, who they think is Crystal Moth) is getting more careful.

Polariton returns to his dorm having learned a lot from the heroes despite his original objective of finding the jewel being inconclusive.  So he shelves that idea in his head for when it might become important later, and starts writing down what he learned about combat (since he’ll forget once he de-akumatizes himself).  Since the jewel search was inconclusive, and Registrar did give him information on Ruth’s classes and social circles, he decides to proceed with his original plan, or rather, setting up his original plan, in his next akumatization, once the opportunity presents itself.  Meanwhile the heroes debate whether to look for Argus, but after Chapter 7’s events, decide not to entertain it.  They instead settle on: if Argus is really going to show up after Chapter 7’s no-show, he can come to them, and they’ll deal with him then.

Post-credits: Nishant hears of a social media clip going around of the battle, and feigns interest.  Now he has a recording he can study, and his private notes.
