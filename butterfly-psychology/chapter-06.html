Chapter 6: Examinator

“You’re so much better than you let yourself believe, Ruth.  Please don’t let anyone make you feel small.” 
“You’re so incredible, Ruth.  I just wish you felt safe enough to show it.”
“Ruth, you’re so sweet and kind to everyone, even to those who don’t appreciate it.  You never deserved what was done to you.”
“You already do so much for all of us, Ruth. Please let us take care of you for once.”
“Seeing you that scared broke my heart, Ruth. You deserve to be yourself.  Please, Ruth?  Please be OK?”
Sanjana’s line was squeezed in the corner.  “We’re so glad you survived!  Please be safe, Ruth?  We love you so much!”
The signatures of Mira, Lucy, Darren, Victor, Sanjana, Paige, Claire, Eric and Max, clustered at the bottom of the card, each in their own unique color.
In the center of the table sat Scarletbug’s magical charm.  Ruth’s soft pink oversized backpack bulged against the leg of her table.  Beside Ruth, Nooroo trembled uncontrollably, unable to speak or even stutter.  Above them, a dark purple akuma fluttered around.

Ruth picked up the magical charm from the table with her left hand, and held up the soft pink stress ball in her right hand.
“Come, my akuma, and destroy this stupid charm,” she commanded.
As the akuma approached the stress ball, the magical charm in Ruth’s hand glowed brightly and a light pink shield formed around the ball, neutralizing the akuma on contact, turning it white.
Ruth grit her teeth, set the charm and stress ball on the table, and looked at Nooroo.  “Nooroo, dark wings rise!”

The camera shows Ruth’s transformation sequence into Crystal Moth.

Crystal Moth held out her left hand for the white butterfly to settle in, and then cupped her right hand around her left hand, with the butterfly inside.  “If Scarletbug could twist her powers to block me, then I can twist mine to break her!” she hissed, squinting her eyes and squeezing her hands tightly around the butterfly as a dark purple aura seeped into the butterfly.  Her eyes snapped open as she opened her palms, allowing the newly created akuma to fly around the room.
“Dark wings fall.”
A shimmer traveled rapidly from Crystal Moth’s toe to her head, and Nooroo emerged from the Butterfly Miraculous brooch as it reverted back to the form of a 🌸 brooch.  Ruth held the magical charm in her left hand and stress ball in her right, and looked at her akuma intently.
“Destroy Scarletbug’s silly little trinket!” Ruth commanded.
As the akuma approached the stress ball, the charm glowed and a pink shield formed around the stress ball.  As the akuma pushed back, it sent ripples through the shield, and quickly drained of color.  Ruth clenched her fists around the charm and stress ball, then shoved them onto her table.
“Nooroo, dark wings rise!”
Nooroo was sucked into the 🌸 brooch, charging it into the usual appearance of the Butterfly Miraculous brooch, as a shimmer traveled from Ruth’s head to her toe, transforming her into Crystal Moth.  Crystal Moth held out her left palm for the white butterfly and cupped her right hand over it.  As she squeezed her hands tightly together, her entire costume glowed an eerie purple.  “He deserves to break!” Crystal Moth hissed.  Sparks flew between her palms as the dark purple aura seeped into the akuma.  “Why won’t anyone just break already?”  When she lifted her hand, the akuma rested in her palm, still sparking as it fluttered away from her palm.
“Dark wings fall.”
A shimmer traveled from Crystal Moth’s toe to her head, and Nooroo emerged from the brooch.  Ruth picked up the charm and stress ball.
“Come megakuma, and render this pathetic trinket defenseless against me!  Do it.  Now,” Ruth commanded.
As the megakuma approached the stress ball in Ruth’s hand, the charm sparked violently as the pink barrier appeared around the stress ball.  The megakuma pushed, sending ripples through the barrier until it shattered, and the charm vaporized out of her hand.  The megakuma merged with the stress ball, turning it pitch black, and a dark purple mist enveloped Ruth.  When it receded, Sentinella stood in her flowing pink dress, holding the blackened stress ball.
“Perfect,” Sentinella said to herself.  She squeezed her eyes shut and grit her teeth, sweeping her arms outward.  But nothing happened.  “Leave me, akuma,” she said with a slightly strained voice.  She staggered back, clutching her head.  Nooroo gulped, peeking at Sentinella from the pencil box he hid in.  “No!  He’s the one who deserves this!” Sentinella snarled.  She looked at the stress ball, and with a violent tear, she ripped it in half, sending tiny pieces of foam all over the floor.  The megakuma flitted out of the toy and the dark purple mist covered Sentinella, and when it receded, Ruth stood in front of the table, holding her torn, mangled stress ball.  She breathed a sigh of relief, and opened her drawer, reaching in and pinning the 🩷 brooch below the 🌸 brooch.  Duusu materialized in front of her and gulped.
“Nooroo, Duusu, dual metamorphosis!” she commanded.

Nooroo and Duusu were sucked into the 🌸 and 🩷 brooches, respectively.  The camera shows Ruth’s transformation sequence into Chrysanthia.

Chrysanthia picked up her cane and smirked at the akuma as it slipped inside.  She plucked a feather from her fan, and squeezed it in her fist.  A dark blue aura seeped into the feather.  When she opened her fist, she pinched the dark blue amok between her fingers and slipped it into her card titled “To Our Sweet and Perfect Ruth” that she held in her left hand, then extended her right hand.
“Oh my amok, bring my creation to life!”

“So when looking at pH in titrations, when do we use Henderson-Hasselbalch vs ICE tables?  I’m a bit confused on when to use what.”

Professor Lansen nodded.  “That’s a very clear and concise explanation.”  He addressed the room.  “As Elliot said, a quick way to determine which method to use is by first figuring out which species dominate.”
Sanjana nodded slightly, then quickly stilled as her eyes darted around the group.  Ruth glanced quickly at Elliot, then down at her notebook, and scooted her chair towards the table so that her body pressed against its edge.

“Go ahead,” Professor Lansen replied.
“So we learned about how we can calculate solubility in pure water from $K_{sp}$, but what if one of the ions was being consumed?  For example, if we had cadmium phosphate in an ammonia buffer holding the pH between the last two $pK_a$’s, how do we calculate the increase in solubility then?”
Professor Lansen nodded at Elliot.  “You’ve combined $K_{sp}$, buffers and Le Chatelier’s principle quite nicely, and your intuition is correct.  But the exact calculation is beyond the scope of this class.”

Professor Lansen nodded at Claire and Ruth.  “Ruth, if you want to say something, it’s OK,” he said gently.

“No need to apologize,” Professor Lansen said warmly.  “In ammonia solution, $\ce{Cd^{2+}}$ does indeed form a complex ion, which gives us another equilibrium to consider.  Again, solving it is much outside the scope of this class.”

$$K_{sp}' = K_{sp} \color{blue}{\left(\frac{1}{\alpha_{\ce{PO4^3-}}} \right)^2} \color{red}{\left(1 + K_f[\ce{NH3}]^4\right)^3}$$ The group blinked at the equation on the page.  “Because of speciation of both cadmium and phosphate, you need to consider the conditional $K_{sp}’$ in terms of total concentrations.  Then you just get the solubility like you would normally…” Ruth’s voice trailed off again.

“I-it’s a distribution fraction,” Ruth replied timidly.  “$1/\alpha$ is just a polynomial in $[\ce{H+}]$ so I thought it made things easier…”

Professor Lansen fell quiet for a moment, then let out a small thoughtful breath.  “Yes,” he said slowly.  “That’s a very solid outline of how an advanced experiment might actually look, once you know the standard reduction potential for $\ce{Cd^{2+}}$.  Your way of getting the precipitation and complexation information out of a single experiment is particularly clever.  Usually, you’d use several experiments.”
