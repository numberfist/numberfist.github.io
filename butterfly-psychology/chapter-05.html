Chapter 5: Sentinella

The hallway outside MIT classroom 66-144 in the ChemE building was mostly quiet.  Mira, her friend Lucy, as well as Sanjana, Victor and Darren walked casually towards the door.  Behind them, Ruth shuffled haltingly with hesitant steps.

“If we make it through this pset by the time we’re done, then I’d be impressed,” Lucy remarked.

“So, usual seats, everyone?” Mira asked as she began reorienting one of the lecture chairs near the center of the room.
“Sure,” Sanjana replied as the group took their seats around the reoriented lecture chairs, swiveling them around to form a circle.  Mira and Lucy sat one seat apart from each other and brought out their laptops from their backpacks, Darren dropped his bag with a soft thud, and Sanjana sat roughly across the empty seat between Mira and Lucy. Ruth timidly shuffled towards the seat between Mira and Lucy, and allowed her backpack to slip off her shoulders.  She hesitantly placed her pencil box on the table in front of her as she sat down, and then her closed notebook.  Ruth’s fingers traced the plastic lock of her pencil box as she unlocked it, revealing the careful assortment of gel pens, before shakily opening her notebook.  Her notes were tiny, dense, multicolored handwriting, with the occasional Lewis structures scattered around.

“And there it is!” Lucy remarked to the group.
“I bet Ruth already did the entire pset in her head,” Mira added.  “Just look at her notes!”
Darren scooted towards Ruth and leaned over towards her notebook curiously.
Ruth’s arms tightened over her notebook as her face flushed.  “I- I’m sorry,” she squeaked.  “I t-thought if I rewrote the textbook I-I’d remember more…” her voice trailed off.
“You don’t need to apologize for being so on top of things,” Mira said encouragingly.  “Rewrote the textbook?  Of course you did, just like always!  And here we are still pretending we understand it.”
“I can’t even read that small,” Darren added.  “How do you write all of that?!  I could never!”
Ruth’s arms slipped from her notebook as they moved to squeeze her body.
“With Ruth here, this pset will be easy,” Sanjana said warmly in Ruth’s direction.  “Speaking of which,” she continued, addressing the group, “What did you guys get for 1a, the $\ce{CH3F}$ structure?  I got this.”  She moved her notebook towards the center of the group, and the others leaned in to look at her structure, which had the C attached to 3H and 1 F, with 3 lone pairs on the F.  Darren and Lucy glanced over.  “Nice!” Lucy exclaimed, “I got that too!”  She looked at Ruth.  “Ruth?” she asked softly.  All heads turned to Ruth, who gave a small nod of her head.  “Yeah, if Ruth agrees, then it must be correct,” Darren confirmed.  Sanjana nodded.
“See?” Mira said softly to Ruth.  “You don’t have to be afraid.  It’s always so much better when you’re here, and that’s a good thing.”  Ruth glanced at the blank space on her page.

The door handle clicked, then turned.  Ruth flinched without looking up.  The door swung open, and Elliot stepped inside with his backpack over his shoulders and notebook in hand.  He paused as he noticed the group.  The moment Ruth glanced at Elliot, her shoulders rose and her eyes dropped instantly to her notebook, as she shakily scooted her chair an inch towards Mira.  Mira’s posture went rigid as she looked tenderly at Ruth, then glared at Elliot.  Lucy’s smile evaporated, and Victor went still as his eyes landed on Elliot.  Sanjana didn’t move, but her eyes flicked once towards Ruth, then away.

“Nice!” Lucy exclaimed enthusiastically, nodding at Ruth.
Sanjana glanced at her page.  “Same here.”
“Yeah, matches mine,” Elliot said neutrally, without opening his notebook.  His shoulders tensed.
